# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive (iOS and Android treated equally; the Expo web build is a development preview only)

## Users

Department responders at Noida International Airport (ARFF, security, medical, terminal, airside and others) and AOCC duty staff away from their desks. They use the app on the move: outdoors on the apron in hard sunlight, in dark control rooms and on night shifts, often gloved, one-handed, and while on a radio.

The defining moment is an alert arriving: the phone wakes, and the person must understand what, where and how long ago within seconds, then respond.

## Product Purpose

ENS Mobile delivers emergency alerts that cannot be missed, captures acknowledgement and responder status, and lets people work their department checklists, raise field reports and follow incidents to closure. It works with poor signal: actions queue on the device and send automatically.

Success means every required responder acknowledges quickly, and AOCC can trust what the app reports.

## Operating Context

- Accounts come from ENS: an administrator creates each user, who signs in with email and password.
- Live and drill incidents can run at the same time.
- The console (SI-ENS-FE) is the sibling surface. A person may see the same incident on both, so the two must speak one visual language.

## Brand Commitments

Inherited from the console: a calm, Apple-like system that reduces cognitive load. The earlier airfield-signage and flight-deck languages are retired.

## Product Principles

1. Emergency first: the most urgent thing on screen is always the most visible thing, and red means an emergency, nothing else.
2. Show what needs attention and summarise the rest.
3. A drill is unmistakable from a live event at a glance.
4. The app never overstates: a queued action says it has not reached AOCC yet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Severity and status are never shown by colour alone. Touch targets are at least 48pt for gloved use. Text scales with system settings. Respect reduced motion. All text meets WCAG AA.
